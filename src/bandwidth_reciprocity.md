# Bandwidth Reciprocity

How complex of an action did you have to take before you were presented with this idea?

Humans can communicate however they see fit. Despite this being technically true, it is generally understood that members of a community are expected to abide by certain unspoken rules.

[Grice's Maxims](https://www.sas.upenn.edu/~haroldfs/dravling/grice.html)

You can explicate these rules if you are the sort of person inclined to do so. One such rule that I try to follow, which may or may not have been obvious to Grice, is thus:

> Listeners shall develop faithful mental models of their interlocutor's ideas.

The fidelity of these models should be limited only by constraints that are (in the present moment) out of the listener's control: the finite human working memory, tiredness, emotional state.
